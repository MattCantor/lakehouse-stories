---
title: 'Carlo the Caterpillar, Drag Queen '
---

Carlo the Caterpillar was thrilled to be on RuPaul’s Drag Race. His drag name was Coco. She had gotten tiny makeup brushes from Tito the Tick, her fellow drag queen, also known as Taylor, who had gotten them from Fiona the Fox. Sadly Taylor had not been selected, but Coco promised to win for her. 
In the first challenge, making the outfit of a real, Royal queen from grocery bags, Coco won! She had a bit of an advantage because she could take little itty bitty pieces from her bags so they wouldn’t look like bags anymore. She was finished very fast, because fun fact: caterpillars are great at sewing. She had lots of time for perfect makeup after. The judges LOVED her style. It took a while for them to see, because they had to get a ladybug cameraman to video her and then have that video on a screen in front of them. But once that was done, they all agreed Coco was awesome! 
Coco moved on to win RuPaul’s drag race. She was given a beautiful, tiny crown. Everyone loved her. She was famous back at the Lakehouse. She went to the Foresthood School for an assembly and everything. 
She even went back on the show to be a guest judge. She just sat on the judges table because if she was on a chair she wouldn’t be able to see. They had allowed her to because she was so amazing!
