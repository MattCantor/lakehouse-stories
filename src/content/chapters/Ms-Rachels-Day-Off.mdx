title: Ms. Rachel’s Day Off
---

Act 1 

Delilah the doe, head of the D.O.E and Dolly’s mom found out that the teachers weren’t getting any days off and were starting to protest. Principal Ralph had been taking all the days off for his vacation in Jamaica. How dare he! Principal Ralph had been keeping it a secret from her, too. This was unprecedented behavior!

Delilah decided to give all three teachers a day off, on the same day, but she needed a substitute. Then she had an idea. Wally, the mayor! He had lots of civil duties as mayor and this was one of them. 

So she called him and basically ordered to do it. She had a very commanding presence. This was the last thing Wally needed. He had his hands full with the FBI. They had gotten statements from 30 chickens about his stealing their friends and family, and now they were investigating him! 

Freddy of the F.B.I stopped by just as he was leaving. 

“Hey Wally.” 

“Hi Freddy.” 

“Where are you going? If it’s in the direction of Mr. Finagan’s coop you have to be accompanied by an agent.” 

“I’m going to the Foresthood School.” 

“AGENTS AT THE READY!” 

“IT”S FOR CIVIL DUTIES!” 

“Fine.” Freddy grumbled. 

Wally jumped. “Wait! I forgot my lunch!” 

“IS IT A CHICKEN SANDWHICH?” 

“Chill out, it’s a bagel.” 

Act 2 

The Foresthood School kids eagerly awaited their substitute. Today they were all in one classroom. Nine kids! One substitute! Nine ages! 

Wally arrived in a full-on trench coat. He was carrying a blackboard. He scribbled POLITICS on it with chalk. “Who knows what this word means?” 

“Dinosaur!” 

“Circulatory!” 

“Insurmountable!” 

“Bagel!” 

“Those four make my lunch!” He replied.
