---
title: Humans of the Lakehouse
synopsis: There are humans in the Lakehouse too!
---

Mr. Finagan 

Mr. Finagan is 60 years old.  He lives near the forest. He likes to fish. He owns chickens. He and his wife, Mrs. Finagan, live close to their nieces and nephew who are always visiting. He used to be mayor but Wally beat him in an election. He is always trying to get Wally and the foxes in trouble for stealing his chickens all the time, but they are too clever for him. 

Jefe Manager 

Jefe is 48. He loves to golf with his son. He owns a mine. He employs miners who look for special rocks and then sell them. He is good friends with Wally. He is a co-owner of the mine, with an angry bear. He employs a young ostrich named Emo who occasionally finds rainbow seashells (which are very rare) with Wally’s moral support, but otherwise makes a big ruckus. He does not question this. 

Nessa and Nancy the Nieces

Nessa and Nancy are 13 years old. They goes to Port Jervis Middle School with their best friend Gigi the Giraffe. They are Mr. Finagan’s nieces. They have a little brother named Nigel. Once, Nessa accidentally used a snail princess as fish bait, but that was when she was, like, six. Plus a buffalo saved the snail. Nessa and Nancy are very friendly with the animals. 

Nigel the Nephew 

Nigel is 5 years old. He is Mr. Finagan’s nephew. He once got enrolled in Foresthood School for a few months, but it wasn’t the right fit. He is always hyper. He loves the forest and all the animals. 

Agent Freddy of the F.B.I 

Freddy is 37. He thought being an F.B.I agent would be more fun but for some reason he never finds anyone guilty. It’s very annoying. 

The Cops 

The Cops are 33 and 30. They are Wally’s besties for life. Wally and the cops go to the pizza shop all the time. The cops are a married couple.
