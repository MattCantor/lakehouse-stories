Lyland Lace the Leopard left the Foresthood School cheerfully. He was so happy to end his day as a teacher and go to sleep. However, Chatt the Cheetah had other plans. 
“Lace?” She asked. 
“Sup,” he answered. 
“Do you know about the stuff eating our trees?”  
“Ya it’s gonna kill us all.” 
“Ok cool. Come to my lab right now.” 
“Wait-”
“That wasn’t a question.” 
Lace slowly walked with her. She showed him a lab. “Get working,” she said. “Discover something.” 
“…’Kay.” 
He worked in the lab for a whole day straight before he figured out….HOW IT BREEDS! “OMG I FIGURED OUT SOEMTHING!” Before he could even finish his sentence, Chatt’s guards threw him in an aircraft carrier! 
“So what’d you figure out?” They said in perfect unison, and then glared at each other. 
He pulled out a dry erase marker and a whiteboard from his pocket. He drew his experiment. 
“Okay…..make a raft powered by this.” 
“What?” 
“Did I pronounce it wrong?” 
“No, Chatt.” 
So he and his new besties for life, the other scientists, started their super exciting project, making a raft powered by what they liked to call Astro-Sap. Astro-Sap was destroying their trees. They planned to send a raft across the lake to the other side, where there were no infected trees. The only problem? 
DUN DUN DUN! The raft wouldn’t have enough Astro-Sap Powered triple A batteries to go both ways. They would send a tiny raft home with their findings….AND DIE ON THE OTHER SIDE. 
“I am so happy I am not on that trip,” Lace said. 
FORESHADOWING FORESHADOWING FORESHADOWING!
